What can a social media post reveal about the person behind the words? Beyond opinions and everyday experiences, the way people write may offer clues about their personality. This paper explores how researchers use these clues to predict personality traits. Based on a structured literature review, different approaches will be presented, ranging from word counts and classical machine learning to transformers and large language models. Although newer models can capture more of the meaning and context in language, their predictions still depend on the available data and trustworthy labels. The review also highlights less explored questions about network participation, explanations and privacy. Together, these findings show what current methods can offer and what needs further research before personality predictions can be trusted completely.
