The renewal need for Norwegian municipal water and wastewater pipes is placed at a cost of an estimated 229 billion NOK by 2045. The actual renewal performed by these municipalities is under one percent of their networks each year. New EU regulations also put additional environmental requirements on the wastewater network, such as reducing overflow discharges, increasing the need for renewal further. Which pipes are renewed therefore affects water loss, discharges to the environment and the use of public funds. Predicting which pipe fails next is a well-studied problem. Language models are also starting to appear in planning tools, but no study has coupled a language model with a failure prediction model on real network data and studied whether planners can trust the result. This paper reports the preliminary project for a master thesis that will design such a system through design science research. A semi-systematic multivocal literature review and interviews with seven informants in four municipalities were used to get an understanding of current practices in Norwegian municipalities and derive requirements for a new system. The findings suggest that the biggest gains will not come from better prediction models. Simple rules based on break history rank pipes as well as machine learning models unless the network is large with long records. Initial tests on open and municipal data point the same way. What municipalities lack is the combination of failure probability with consequence, capacity and coordination with other works together with a way to check what a suggestion builds on. No informant wanted a system that decides on its own. These findings give six requirements, among them traceability, advisory use and municipal control of data. The goal of the thesis is therefore to design and evaluate an agent-based decision support system where suggestions are explainable and traceable to municipalities' own data and plans, and to measure if this traceability gives planners trust that follows the quality of the suggestions.
